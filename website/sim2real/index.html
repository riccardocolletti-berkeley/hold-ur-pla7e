Sim2Real

The controllers deployed on the live UR7e arm. For every reference trajectory the live overhead camera is paired with the RViz mirror of the same instant: a real-world view of the plate and the ball, alongside the joint state and the commanded plate frame reconstructed from the transform tree.

The four reference shapes tracked on the live arm. Each panel shows the plate frame, the ArUco corners, the reference trace, and the ball.

§ 1Tracking a circle

The reference is a 10 cm-radius circle traversed at a fixed period. The live overhead camera frames the plate and the ball; the RViz visualiser reconstructs the joint state, the commanded plate frame, and the reference path that the controller is chasing.

§ 2Tracking a figure-of-eight

A Lissajous figure-of-eight: the trajectory crosses itself at the centre, so the ball passes through the same point twice per loop in opposite directions.

§ 3Tracking a square

A square reference: four straight edges joined by sharp corners.

§ 4User-drawn reference

The designer tool lets an operator sketch an arbitrary closed shape on a tablet; the sketch is resampled to a smooth reference trajectory and uploaded to the controller, which then drives the arm along it.
